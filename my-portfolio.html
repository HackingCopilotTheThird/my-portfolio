What Is Geometry Dash?

Geometry Dash is a popular 2D rhythm-based action platformer video game created by Swedish developer Robert Topala and published by RobTop Games. Released initially on August 13, 2013, the game challenges players to guide a customizable icon (starting as a small square cube) automatically moving from left to right through music-synced, obstacle-filled levels. Here, the game switches to a multitude of different gamemode. For example, there is wave, ball, ship, swing, spider, and more coming soon (hopefully)
