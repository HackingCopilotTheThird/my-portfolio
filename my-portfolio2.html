What makes a Top Player?

A top player is someone who is SO good at the game that they are on a different level than the average playerbase. Some notable examples include the following, (Zoink, wPopoff, Trick, Doggie, etc.) There are a multitude of leaderboards, but the most notable is Pointercrate. There is a system called Pointercrate Player Stats Viewer.

1. Zoink

When people talk about the absolute GOAT of Geometry Dash, one name sits right at the summit: Zoink, the undisputed king of the demonlist who completely rewrote what the community thought was humanly possible through legendary verifications and completions like Acheron, Avernus, Tidal Wave, and Thinking Space II, and Artemis 13 to name a few. He stands entirely alone at the top after spaceuk's legacy collapsed due to botted runs. A human beat a literal HACKER who was hacking all of his completions. And Zoink still came out on top. Dominating deep into 2026, Zoink continues to push the absolute bleeding edge of human skill by conquering brutal new top-tier extremes, such as his grueling ~30,000-attempt verification of MindCap's ORBIT in February 2026, while comfortably holding the #1 spot on the Pointercrate individual stats viewer with a massive lead over competitors like wPopoff, zLevii, and Trick.
