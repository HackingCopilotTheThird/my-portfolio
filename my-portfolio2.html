Top Players

What makes a Top Player?

A top player is someone who is SO good at the game that they are on a different level than the average playerbase. Some notable examples include the following, (Zoink, wPopoff, Trick, Doggie, etc.) There are a multitude of leaderboards, but the most notable is Pointercrate. There is a system called Pointercrate Player Stats Viewer.

1. Zoink

When people talk about the absolute GOAT of Geometry Dash, one name sits right at the summit: Zoink, the undisputed king of the demonlist who completely rewrote what the community thought was humanly possible through legendary verifications and completions like Acheron, Avernus, Tidal Wave, and Thinking Space II, and Artemis 13 to name a few. He stands entirely alone at the top after spaceuk's legacy collapsed due to botted runs. A human beat a literal HACKER who was hacking all of his completions. And Zoink still came out on top. Dominating deep into 2026, Zoink continues to push the absolute bleeding edge of human skill by conquering brutal new top-tier extremes, such as his grueling ~30,000-attempt verification of MindCap's ORBIT in February 2026, while comfortably holding the #1 spot on the Pointercrate individual stats viewer with a massive lead over competitors like wPopoff, zLevii, and Trick.

2. wPopoff

The current second best player is wPopoff. He is one of the biggest underdogs in GD history, completing Acheron, the #1 demon at the time. And this was just the beginning to his rise of fame. From here, he proceeded to slaughter the demonlist, completing list demons left and right. Soon, many people called him a potential rival to Zoink, the #1 Player. His true skill of test was when he was competing with Trick for first victor on Tidal Wave, just after it got rated and placed on the demon list at #1. Both were making insane progress, one-upping each other almomst every other day. When the race was at it's peak, both wPopoff and Trick having 95% on Tidal Wave, Trick barely grasped the first victor title of the level. 3 days later though, Popoff finally beat Tidal Wave. Since then, he has secured himself as the second-best player, with people even debating if he is better than Zoink. His list of accomplishments just keep on growing. Just in 2026 alone, he verified the top 1 level, Society, and beaten the newest top 1, Grief.

3. zLevii

New Zealand player zLevii climbed to the #3 spot on the Pointercrate stats viewer in a extremly unique way (in my opinion). He obtained this spot in July of 2026, from an unspotabble grind of multiple list completions. After being an underdog in 2023-2024, he broke into many players eyes as a future top player of fluing "Spectre", a top 10 list demon at the time. There were 5 other victors of the level, but the way he did it is the most iconic. He only played this demon for FOUR days, racking up 6,500 attempts and fluked the level from 45%. But this year was definetly his best year on Geometry Dash. An underdog in becoming the second victor of Flamewall, he continued gaining attention from the community. In July of this year, he surpassed rivals such as Netermind and Trick (a top-3 player for years) by submitting 4 list completions (Snowbound, Being Home, Trickshot, and Between Duality) on the same day, finally passing Trick and earning his place in the top 3. He became the highest-ranked Oceanic GD player ever. He has become the first non-american top 3 player in years, before cementing his rank with ~50 list completions, beating The Bloop and getting 75% on Tidal Wave

4. Trick

Trick carved out his legacy as one of the most dominant figures in Geometry Dash history by starting a legendary run of verifications and first victors. After rising to fame by verifying Firework in mid-2021, he cemented his status by becoming the first victor of iconic top-tier extremes like Abyss of Darkness (2022) and Tidal Wave (early 2024), ultimately getting the #1 spot on the Pointercrate Demonlist stats viewer from June to September 2024. Following periods of burnout, consideration of quitting top play, and a brief hiatus, Trick mounted major comebacks, climaxing with his grueling completion milestones such as beating the former Top 1 Thinking Space II as its second victor and beating Flamewall, which repeatedly secured his holding near the absolute peak of the leaderboard and set the blueprint for modern top-play excellence.

5. Netermind

Russian player Netermind cemented his status as one of the best and skilled competitors in Geometry Dash history through an extraordinary completetion of high-tier list grinds and massive difficulty completions. Rising steadily through the Pointercrate Demonlist stats viewer, Netermind made his standing among the best by tackling legendary extremes like Thinking Space II, Amethyst, Flamewall, and Tidal Wave, while maintaining an immense volume of Main and Extended List completions that placed him firmly in the upper echelons of competitive play. His relentless consistency and deep mastery over complex wave, ship, and timing gameplay have made him a pillar of modern community progression and a formidable rival on the global leaderboard.
