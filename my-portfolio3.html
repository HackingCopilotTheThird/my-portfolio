Top Levels

What makes a Top Level?

A Top 1 Demon is the absolute pinnacle of human skill at the time that "demon" is top 1. Most Top 1's have hundereds of frame-perfects, weird hitboxes, and a history of brutal fails. Not to mention the attempt count :/. To claim a spot as the top 1 demon, it must be more difficult than all of the other demons that are on the Pointercrate Demon List at the time it got verified. The player must have video proof with clicks, sounds, and other requirements to be accepted because many hacked levels used to dominate the demon list.

1. Grief

One of the most anticipated top 1s in the game's history, with a verification process spanning 396 streams and a cumulative playtime of 922 hours; currently the hardest level in the game. Verified by Doggie after an exhaustive two-year grind of roughly 440,000 attempts, including a nearly year-long wall at 80% and two heartbreaking crashes at 97%, its completion drew tens of thousands of live viewers.This level immediately redefined the absolute ceiling of human skill in Geometry Dash.

2. Artemis 13

A monochrome design level featuring many clubstep monsters scattered throughout. It was originally harder before being nerfed by Zoink following a controversy surrounding the original host. Features an extremely hard ship at 19%. Set to a frantic, intense soundtrack, this infamous Extreme Demon blends classic 1.9-era atmospheric theming with relentless timings and tight timings. Despite the post-controversy nerfs, it remains a brutal endurance test where a single lapse in precision, especially during its notorious early ship instantly ends a run.

3. Society

The sequel to Escalator and a nerfed impossible level. Featuring a dreamcore/weirdcore aesthetic, it is filled with references and explores themes of mental instability. Hosted by Neomarbilan and verified by wPopoff after roughly 75,000 attempts, Society became the first Top 1 Extreme Demon to utilize Update 2.2 features, dethroning Thinking Space II on the Demonlist after ~1 year. Blending Slaughterhouse-inspired gameplay with eerie visuals and subliminal imagery, it stands out as one of the most atmospheric and psychologically heavy levels to ever claim the #1 spot.

4. Thinking Space II

An official sequel to Thinking Space and former top 1. Unlike the original, its decoration is in a glow design style, but it's more similar in gameplay as it has several gameplay references to the original and is also extremely end carried. The first top 1 in many years that was not built intentionally to be top 1. Hosted by CairoX and verified by Zoink after roughly 53,000 attempts, including a devastating death at 95%, it took over the #1 spot on the Demonlist from Tidal Wave in 2025. Built with claustrophobic corridors and a punishing final jump that ramps up the nerve control to its absolute peak, it proved that an accidental Top 1 could still rival the hardest intentionally built megacollabs in Geometry Dash history.

5. Amethyst

Amethyst is an unofficial sequel to The Golden and a former Top 1 Extreme Demon in Geometry Dash. Retaining the atmospheric wilderness aesthetic of its predecessor while shifting to a striking purple crystal theme, it replaces pure memory gameplay with tight wave corridors and frame-perfect timings across most gamemode. Hosted by iMist and verified by wPopoff after roughly 67,000 attempts, it claimed the #1 spot on the Demonlist in 2025, standing out as one of the most technically demanding and visually cohesive megacollabs in the game's history. This also dethroned TIDAL WAVEE
