Top Levels

What makes a Top Level?

A Top 1 Demon is the absolute pinnacle of human skill at the time that "demon" is top 1. Most Top 1's have hundereds of frame-perfects, weird hitboxes, and a history of brutal fails. Not to mention the attempt count :/. To claim a spot as the top 1 demon, it must be more difficult than all of the other demons that are on the Pointercrate Demon List at the time it got verified. The player must have video proof with clicks, sounds, and other requirements to be accepted because many hacked levels used to dominate the demon list.

1. Grief

One of the most anticipated top 1s in the game's history, with a verification process spanning 396 streams and a cumulative playtime of 922 hours; currently the hardest level in the game.

2. Artemis 13

A monochrome design level featuring many clubstep monsters scattered throughout. It was originally harder before being nerfed by Zoink following a controversy surrounding the original host. Features an extremely hard ship at 19%.

3. Society

The sequel to Escalator and a nerfed impossible level. Featuring a dreamcore/weirdcore aesthetic, it is filled with references and explores themes of mental instability.

4. Thinking Space II

An official sequel to Thinking Space and former top 1. Unlike the original, its decoration is in a glow design style, but it's more similar in gameplay as it has several gameplay references to the original and is also extremely end carried. The first top 1 in many years that was not built intentionally to be top 1.

5. Amethyst

A former top 1, dethroning Tidal Wave after two years. It's inspired by The Golden, including a cave decoration with rocks, geodes, and purple and pink crystals. The gameplay consists of a lot of extremely hard waves and ship gameplay, similar to The Golden but with an extension and a random 240 frame perfect first click.
